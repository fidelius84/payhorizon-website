1. Who We Are

Payhorizon Limited is an independent merchant services broker registered in England and Wales. We introduce businesses to payment providers. We are the data controller and are registered with the ICO under No. ZC038563.

2. Data We Collect

Directly from you: Name, date of birth, identity documents, contact details, business information (name, company number, VAT number, turnover, nature of business), bank details, processing statements, and communication records.

Automatically: IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, and cookie identifiers (see Cookie Policy).

From third parties: Companies House (director verification), credit reference agencies, fraud prevention databases, and acquiring banks (account status, compliance).
