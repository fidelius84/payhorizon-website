Our matching process

How we choose
the right acquirer

01
Sector risk appetite

Every acquirer has a different appetite for different industries. We match your MCC to the acquirer most likely to approve — and approve at the best rate.

02
Volume & rate competitiveness

Rate bands vary by acquirer and by monthly card volume. We identify where your volume sits in each acquirer's pricing matrix and pick the most competitive.

03
Terminal compatibility

Different acquirers support different hardware. We ensure the terminal or gateway you want is available from the acquirer we're recommending — no surprises.

04
Approval likelihood

Some acquirers have stricter underwriting on new businesses, directors with thin credit files, or certain sectors. We route your application to maximise your chance of approval first time.
