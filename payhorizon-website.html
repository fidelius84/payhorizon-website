FAQ

Is this a loan?

+
No. A Merchant Cash Advance is not a loan — it's an advance against your future card sales. There's no fixed repayment date and no fixed monthly payment. You repay a percentage of what you take each day, so repayments flex with your revenue.

Does it affect my credit score?

+
The initial assessment uses a soft credit check only — no impact on your credit score. A hard check may be required before final approval on larger advances.

What is a factor rate?

+
A factor rate is the cost of the advance expressed as a multiplier. For example, a factor rate of 1.20 on a £10,000 advance means you repay £12,000 in total. Factor rates range from 1.03 to 1.30 depending on your profile.

How much can I borrow?

+
Up to 2× your average monthly card revenue, to a maximum of £1,000,000. Minimum advance is £5,000.

What can I use the funding for?

+
Anything business-related — stock purchase, equipment, refurbishment, marketing, hiring staff, covering a cash flow gap. There are no restrictions on how you use the funds.
