Who we are

A broker that works
for the merchant

Payhorizon Limited is an independent merchant services broker registered in England and Wales. We are not a bank, an acquirer, or tied to any single payment provider.

We operate as an introducer under M World Business Solutions Limited (MWBS), an FCA-regulated firm (FRN: 1003907). This means every business we introduce connects with a properly regulated acquiring institution — with all the consumer protections that brings.

Our panel of six acquiring institutions — AIB Merchant Services, Elavon, Trust Payments, emerchantpay, Worldpay, and payabl. — covers the full spectrum of UK and international merchant needs. We choose the best fit for each merchant's profile, not the one that pays us the most.
