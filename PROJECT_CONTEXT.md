## What We Sell

Payhorizon operates as a reseller/ISO under MWBS (mwbsolutions.co.uk). MWBS provides us with:

- Card-present (CP) terminal acquiring via **AIB, Elavon, Trust Payments, emerchantpay, Worldpay, myPOS**
- E-commerce / virtual terminal (CNP) gateway solutions
- EPOS software (Register by Smart Volution; POSitive; Clover)
- Hardware (Pax, Ingenico, Castles, Verifone, myPOS, Clover, Worldpay DX8000)

- Open Banking payment journeys (via SOTpay / Gala Technology)

- PSP Angels global/crypto acquiring solutions (high-risk, offshore, niche)

## Product Summary

### 1. Card-Present Merchant Services
Terminals from multiple manufacturers (Pax A920 Pro, Pax A77, Ingenico Move/Desk, Castles Saturn/Vega, Verifone V200c/V240m, Clover Flex/Station, Worldpay DX8000, myPOS Go/Carbon/Slim/Combo).
Acquirers: AIB, Elavon, Trust Payments, emerchantpay, Worldpay, myPOS (see acquirer-device matrix in FACTS.md).

### 2. E-Commerce / Virtual Terminal
Payment gateways via MWBS partner acquirers. Monthly fee + per-transaction fee model. Low, medium, and high-risk tiers available.

### 3. EPOS Solutions
Register (Smart Volution) - Android EPOS with Lite/Core/Plus licensing. Clover software-as-a-service plans. POSitive payment application for terminals.

### 4. Merchant Cash Advance (MCA)
Short-term business funding repaid from a percentage of daily card turnover. £5k--£1m advances. Funded typically within 3--4 days. Pre-approved for Elavon merchants via MWBS Partner Portal. MCA provider: Recap.

### 5. Open Banking
SOTpay (Gala Technology) pay-by-link Open Banking integration for phone/MOTO payments. Reduces card scheme costs and chargebacks.

### 6. Global / High-Risk Processing (PSP Angels)
Offshore / high-risk acquiring for crypto merchants, global gaming, adult, FX, etc. Separate T&C and pricing. Operated via pspangels.com.
