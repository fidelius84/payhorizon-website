## 2. Qualification Questions

**Card-present / EPOS:**
1. Are you currently accepting card payments? (If yes: who is your provider, and when does your contract expire?)
2. What is your approximate monthly card turnover?
3. How many terminals do you need, and do you need them mobile, countertop, or both?
4. Do you need EPOS/stock management, or just card acceptance?
5. Do you take any mail order or telephone payments?

**MCA:**
1. Do you currently accept card payments and have a merchant ID?
2. What is your monthly card revenue approximately?
3. Have you been trading for at least 3 months?
4. Do you have any current business finance arrangements in place?
5. Are you looking for short-term funding to invest in equipment, marketing, or cash flow?

**E-Commerce / Open Banking:**
1. Do you have a website? Are you currently taking online payments?
2. Do you take any phone orders?
3. Are you experiencing chargebacks or fraud on card payments?

## 3. Common Objections and Compliant Responses

**"I'm happy with my current provider."**
> Response: "That's great. We work with multiple acquiring banks so we can often offer a like-for-like comparison. When does your current contract expire? If you're within 3 months of the end, it's worth having a quote ready so you're not auto-renewed at current rates."

**"Your rates sound higher than what I pay now."**
> Response: "Rates depend on card mix, volume, and business type -- they're not always comparable at face value. We can do a full cost-of-acceptance analysis on your last statement if you'd like to see an accurate like-for-like."

**"I've never heard of [acquirer name]."**
> Response: "All of the acquiring banks we work through are FCA-authorised and fully regulated in the UK. [Name] processes billions in card payments annually. We can share their credentials."

**"What if I have a chargeback problem?"**
> Response: "Chargeback handling is managed by the acquiring bank. The agreement you sign is directly with them, and they provide dedicated dispute resolution support. We can also introduce you to Open Banking for MOTO payments, which eliminates chargeback risk on phone orders entirely."

**"I'm a high-risk business -- can you still help?"**
> Response: "Possibly, yes. We work with specialist providers for higher-risk sectors. To give you accurate guidance, I'd need to understand your business category and monthly volumes. High-risk onboarding takes longer and involves additional due diligence, but we've placed merchants successfully in many sectors."
*(Do not promise approval. Refer to MWBS High Risk team for assessment.)*

**"I don't want a rolling reserve."**
> Response: "Rolling reserves are typically only applied to higher-risk business types or new businesses without processing history. For standard low-risk merchants with a clean trading history, they are generally not required. We'll be transparent about what the acquirer requires before you sign anything."

## 4. Suggested Outreach Sequence

### Cold outreach (LinkedIn or email -- new prospect)

**Touch 1 (Day 1) -- Initial connection/email:**
Subject: Card payments for [business type] -- quick question
Body: Short, 3--4 sentences. Reference their business sector. Ask one qualifying question (e.g. "Are you currently locked into a contract?"). No rates in first contact.

**Touch 2 (Day 4) -- Value-add:**
Share one relevant piece of information (e.g. "We work with [6 acquiring banks] so you're not locked into one rate -- useful if your volume changes"). Offer a free statement review.

**Touch 3 (Day 9) -- Soft ask:**
"Happy to run a quick comparison on your current deal -- no obligation. Takes about 10 minutes on a call."

**Touch 4 (Day 16) -- Final:**
Last contact before cooling off. Restate the specific benefit relevant to their sector (e.g. "Most [hospitality] businesses we work with save on their monthly fees within the first year").

### Warm referral or inbound lead
Go straight to qualification call. Use the qualification questions above. Have a quote ready using the MWBS Partner Portal within 24 hours of the call.

### MCA cross-sell (existing Elavon merchant)
1. Check the MWBS Partner Portal for pre-approved MCA offers.
2. Send the branded MCA offer email directly from the portal.
3. Follow up within 48 hours by phone to explain the repayment structure.
4. Offer remains valid for 7 working days.

## 5. Key Differentiators to Lead With

- **Multi-acquirer access:** We are not tied to one bank. We match the merchant to the most appropriate acquirer for their sector and volumes.
- **Transparent quoting:** All fees disclosed upfront in the quote. No hidden costs.
- **Hardware flexibility:** Purchase outright or consider managed options; partner-branded terminals available.
- **MCA add-on:** For existing card merchants, we can offer same-week short-term funding with no personal guarantee (subject to underwriting).
- **Open Banking:** For MOTO merchants, Open Banking removes chargeback risk entirely.

## 6. Things NOT to Say

- Do not say "guaranteed approval" for any product.
- Do not quote specific rates from memory -- always generate a quote from the MWBS portal.
- Do not describe ourselves as an acquirer or regulated payment institution.
- Do not promise settlement timings without confirming the acquirer and risk tier.
- Do not advise on whether a merchant's business is "definitely" low or high risk -- refer to the acquirer's underwriting team.
