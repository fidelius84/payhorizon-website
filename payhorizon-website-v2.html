FAQ

Is this a loan?

+
No. A Merchant Cash Advance is not a loan — it's an advance against your future card sales. There's no fixed repayment date. You repay a percentage of what you take each day, so repayments flex with your revenue.

Does it affect my credit score?

+
The initial assessment uses a soft credit check only — no impact on your score. A hard check may be required on larger advances before final approval.

What is a factor rate?

+
A factor rate is the cost expressed as a multiplier. A factor rate of 1.20 on a £10,000 advance means you repay £12,000 total. Rates range from 1.03 to 1.30 depending on your profile.

What can I use the funding for?

+
Anything business-related — stock, equipment, refurbishment, marketing, staff, cash flow. No restrictions on use.

Our Story

Payhorizon was founded by people who had been on the other side of the table — and didn't like what they found there.

The problem with the payments market

Most businesses sign up to a merchant services provider without fully understanding what they're agreeing to. The pricing is opaque. The contract is long. The support, once you're on board, often disappears.

We experienced this ourselves. We watched businesses overpay for years because nobody explained what IC++ pricing was, or that their sector qualified for better rates, or that a different acquirer would be a better fit.

We also saw a gap at the other end of the market. High-volume operators — transport networks, parking companies, councils — were being handled by the same tied resellers as everyone else. Their volumes deserved a specialist. They rarely got one.

So we built Payhorizon: an independent broker with no allegiance to any single acquirer, no opaque pricing, and a genuine focus on getting the right result for each merchant.
