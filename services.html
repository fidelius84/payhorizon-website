06 — Business Funding

Funding that moves
with your revenue

A Merchant Cash Advance gives you a lump sum repaid as a small percentage of daily card takings. No fixed monthly payment. No fixed term. Quiet months cost you less automatically — making it one of the most flexible forms of business finance available.

→£5,000 to £500,000 depending on card turnover
→Repayment adjusts automatically with trading volume
→Decisions typically within 24–48 hours
→No fixed term — repaid when card turnover repays it
→Use for fit-out, equipment, stock, marketing, or cash flow

Merchant Cash Advances are not regulated credit products. Terms set by the funding provider.
